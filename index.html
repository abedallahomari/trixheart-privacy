Trixheart Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 (advertising and rankings added)

Trixheart ("the app") is a card game published by OMARI. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. Contact: abedallahomari100@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your display name, settings, language, level, rating, coins, shop items, achievements, tips you have seen, a saved practice match and, if you are in an online match, a short-lived reconnect token are stored locally on your device. Practice games against bots do not send any data anywhere.

Sign-in

You can play as a guest, or sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook. If you sign in, the provider shares your name, a unique account ID and your profile photo with the app through Firebase Authentication. We use them only to show your name and recognise your account. We do not receive your password.

Online play

When you play online or at a private table, the app sends your display name, a random device identifier, the cards you play and chat phrases you choose to other players in the same match through Google Firebase Realtime Database (or a game server operated by us). These messages are used only to run the match and are deleted when the match ends or shortly afterwards. Chat is limited to preset phrases and emojis. You can mute chat and report a player from the in-game chat panel.

Rankings

If you play online matches run by our game server, we store a ranking record: your display name, the country you choose, a random device identifier (with a secret that proves the record is yours), your ranking points (all-time and per week) and the number of matches and wins. It is shown to other players on the Players, My country and Countries boards (name, country and points only). Points are calculated by the server from matches it ran; they cannot be edited by players. You can change your country once every 30 days. Deleting your account in Settings → Privacy and data also deletes your ranking record from the server.

Advertising

The Android and iOS apps show ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear as an optional "watch a video" reward, as a short ad between matches (never during a hand), and as a small banner on the menu and shop screens. The web version has no ads.

To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not give AdMob your name, email or gameplay. Ad requests are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and we block sensitive ad categories such as dating, alcohol, gambling and sexual and reproductive health.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy and data → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; on iPhone you can turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we do not do

The app contains no analytics SDKs and no real-money purchases. Coins are virtual and can only be earned by playing or watching reward videos. We do not sell your information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Privacy and data → Delete my account and data. This erases everything stored on your device and deletes your sign-in account. You can also request deletion by emailing abedallahomari100@gmail.com and we will erase any online data associated with your account within 30 days. Uninstalling the app also removes the local data.

Security

Connections to Firebase use HTTPS/TLS. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not use a name that reveals sensitive information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
